| E1 | Contrast ratio = contrast volume ÷ kidney function | Registry analysis of ~59,000 PCIs: risk of contrast nephropathy (a creatinine rise of 0.5 mg/dL or more) and dialysis rose at a ratio of 2 or more and sharply at 3 or more, using creatinine clearance. | Gurm HS et al., J Am Coll Cardiol 2011 (via TCTMD report) | Supported; **uses creatinine clearance, not eGFR** |
