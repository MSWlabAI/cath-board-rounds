| E1 | Contrast ratio = contrast volume ÷ kidney function | Registry analysis of ~59,000 PCIs: risk of contrast nephropathy and dialysis was flat below a ratio of 2 and rose sharply above 3; the authors advised keeping contrast below 3 times, ideally 2 times, the creatinine clearance. The ratio uses a creatinine clearance, not eGFR. (A later Michigan analysis used the Cockcroft-Gault equation; which equation the 2011 paper used is not confirmed here.) The plan therefore shows a Cockcroft-Gault ratio beside the eGFR ratio. | Gurm HS et al., J Am Coll Cardiol 2011 (via TCTMD and later reports; re-searched 2026-11-06) | Supported; **uses creatinine clearance, not eGFR** |
| E2 | Proposed cap 3.7 | In 3,179 PCIs a contrast volume ÷ creatinine clearance of 3.7 or more was an independent predictor of an early abnormal creatinine rise (C-statistic about 0.69). Other studies proposed 2.62 and about 3. | Laskey WK et al., Am Heart J 2007; reviews (re-searched 2026-11-06) | Supported as one published cutoff on creatinine clearance, not a guideline limit |
| E3 | No guideline contrast cap | A search found no ACC/AHA guideline text setting a ratio limit; this row supports the absence of a published cap, not any threshold. | search | Absence of a cap (not a supporting source) |
| E4 | AKI by creatinine: rise of 0.3 mg/dL or more within 48 h, or 1.5 times baseline or more within 7 days | KDIGO definition (stage 1). A third criterion uses urine output. | KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for AKI, 2012 | Supported (several reproductions agree) |
| E5 | CCTA stenosis: 70% or more is severe; left main 50% or more is category 4B | CAD-RADS 2.0: 3 is 50 to 69%, 4A is 70 to 99% in one or two vessels, 4B is left main 50% or more (some sources say more than 50%) or three-vessel 70% or more, 5 is a total occlusion. The categories are set per patient by the most severe stenosis, not per lesion and not by calcium. | CAD-RADS 2.0 (SCCT/ACC/ACR/NASCI, 2022) via PubMed-indexed summaries and Radiopaedia (full text not opened) | Supported by secondary sources |
| E6 | Intermediate CCTA stenosis needs more testing | The 2024 ESC chronic coronary syndromes guideline: an intermediate CCTA stenosis may not be functionally significant, so stress imaging or CT-FFR is recommended to assess it, and invasive angiography should assess intermediate stenoses with FFR or iFR before revascularisation. **The 50 to 69% band edges in this entry come from CAD-RADS category 3 (E5), not from the ESC text**, which I could not confirm gives a percentage range. | 2024 ESC CCS guideline (**primary**, section 3.3, and key-point summaries re-searched 2026-11-06); 2021 AHA/ACC chest pain guideline (summaries) | Primary for the functional-assessment point; the band edges are CAD-RADS 3 |
| E7 | Left main: CABG Class 1; PCI Class 2a in selected patients; CABG preferred when complexity is high | Sections 7.1.3, 7.1.4, 8.1.1 as reported by a case series. Left main disease is a Heart Team factor. | 2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI revascularization guideline (via a JACC Case Reports series) | Supported by one secondary source |
| E8 | Multivessel disease with low EF: CABG over medical therapy | EF of 35% or lower: Class 1 for CABG in suitable stable patients; EF 35 to 50%: Class 2a (sections 7.1.1, 7.1.2). | same | Supported by one secondary source |
| E9 | Intravascular imaging for left main or complex stenting | IVUS Class 2a for stent guidance, particularly left main or complex stenting; also reasonable for intermediate left main stenosis. | 2021 ACC/AHA/SCAI guideline (slides, SCAI page) | Supported by secondary sources; the exact levels need checking |
| E10 | Hemodynamic support for high-risk PCI | The consensus says support devices can help in high-risk PCI (multivessel, left main, last conduit), particularly if inoperable or with severely reduced EF. **It gives no EF cutoff.** My 35% question is a trial-population heuristic (PROTECT II enrolled around EF 30 to 35%). | 2015 SCAI/ACC/HFSA/STS consensus; PROTECT II | Partly supported; the 35% is not a guideline cutoff |
| E11 | Metformin and contrast | The ACR criterion this entry uses: metformin and iodinated contrast need attention when there is AKI or an eGFR below 30. Summaries add that the ACR text names arterial procedures that could send emboli to the renal arteries (first-pass renal exposure); a 2021 meta-analysis found no need to withhold above eGFR 60 and too little evidence at 30 to 60; the European and Royal College of Radiologists wording differs. I could not open the ACR manual itself (the server redirected), so the tag covers only AKI or eGFR below 30 and does not decide arterial first-pass cases. | ACR Manual on Contrast Media (**primary not opened**); a 2021 European Radiology meta-analysis; pharmacy guideline comparison (re-searched 2026-11-06) | Supported by summaries; societies differ |
| E12 | Anticoagulant (apixaban) interruption before a procedure | **Primary text read (2021 EHRA practical guide, open-access manuscript, Figure 14).** The default interruption for apixaban, edoxaban and rivaroxaban is at least 24 h before a low-bleeding-risk procedure and at least 48 h before a high-bleeding-risk one when CrCl is 30 or more, longer at lower CrCl; the guide says the defaults may need individual adaptation, that minor-risk procedures can be done at trough, and it does not class a catheterisation in the text I read. For acute coronary syndromes it describes delaying non-urgent PCI and, for urgent PCI, approaching it like primary PCI (radial access, heparin regardless of the last DOAC dose). The `EHRA DOAC` tag therefore names only the interval for a planned elective procedure. | 2021 EHRA practical guide on NOACs in AF (Steffel et al., EP Europace 2021) | Primary for the interval; cath classification is not in the guide |
| E13 | High-risk chest pain goes to invasive angiography | High-risk features include new LV systolic dysfunction and moderate to severe ischemia on testing. No EF cutoff given in the summaries. | 2021 AHA/ACC chest pain guideline (summaries) | Supported qualitatively |

The recommendation tables themselves were image placeholders, so classes and levels were not readable. | 2024 ESC CCS guideline (**primary**, sections 3.3.3 to 3.3.5) | Primary; classes and levels not read |
